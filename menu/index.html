A little glossary

Words from home

Bāyit

Hebrew for home. The name, and the idea behind everything we do.

Schmear

Yiddish for a generous spread - usually soft cheese - on a bagel. Ours come plain, seaweed, seasonal, or vegan beetroot & tahini.

Tzimmes

Yiddish for sweet. A traditional dish of soft braised carrots, which we turn into a vegan bagel.

Salt beef

The deli classic. Ours is made for us by Curing Rebels from pasture-reared retired dairy cows, in strictly limited weekly amounts.

Feeding a crowd?

Platters for the office, bagels for your café, or a dozen to collect on Saturday. Give us three days’ notice and we’ll have them ready.
