How we make them

Three days, start to finish

There are no shortcuts to a good bagel. Every one takes three days from mixing to the counter - which is also why we ask for three days’ notice on orders.

Day one

Mix

Organic, stone-milled flour meets our rye sourdough starter. The dough is mixed in small batches in our little kitchen in the market.

Day two

Rest

Shaped by hand and left to take its time. A long, slow rest gives the flavour and the dense, chewy-but-not-heavy bite we’re after.

Day three

Boil & bake

Boiled, topped and baked that morning - plain, poppy seed, sesame or honey & caraway - then filled to order from 10am.

Bāyit is Hebrew for home

A brother and sister, a little kitchen and a lot of care

Bāyit is run by siblings Oli and Abi. Oli has spent more than a decade cooking and baking in kitchens from London to New Zealand and Italy. Abi grows herbs and leaves on her allotment in Hollingdean, forages the coast every week and makes the seasonal cordial.

Together they opened Unit 26 in Brighton’s Open Market in February 2025, with one simple rule: no corners cut - on the plate, in the market and in this world.

To us, hospitality means home, and we treat our space and our visitors with the warmth we feel when we think of the value of our home - on the plate, in the market and in this world.
