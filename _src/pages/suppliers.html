Our suppliers

Ethical, local, high-quality sourcing is fundamental to how we operate

Scroll down to learn more about the suppliers that help us maintain that goal.

Sussex Surplus

sussexsurplus.org ↗

A community-embedded social enterprise, they transform surplus food into long-life products and affordable meals; creating paid entry-level positions for young people who face employment barriers while they’re at it.

Working with Sussex Surplus means that we are not only supporting an excellent initiative in reducing waste and providing for the community, but also grants us an ever-changing pantry of delicious pickles, ferments and preserves.

Curing Rebels

curingrebels.com ↗

Locally based in Hove Lagoon, we love working with a company whose passion for high welfare sustainable produce shines through their work. Their salmon comes from Wester Ross and their salt beef is made uniquely for us from pasture-reared retired dairy cows.

We buy a strictly limited quantity of beef weekly, to ensure a minimal impact while delivering a product sourced and reared with care every step of the way.

Hodmedod’s

hodmedods.co.uk ↗

Hodmedod’s produce over 100 minimally processed wholefoods - all from diverse crops, grown in ways that are better for soil, climate and communities.

We buy many of our grains, seeds and pulses from them, revelling in their UK-grown provenance, exciting range and high quality product.

Shrub

shrub.london ↗

Supplying the best quality fresh produce available in the East Sussex/Kent region, working directly with over fifty specially selected market gardens and farms.

Their reputation comes from their focus on promoting seasonality, quality, regionality and soil health.

Gilchesters Organics

gilchesters.com ↗

British, organic, stone-milled flour - this is a beautiful, beautiful company, and its heart shines through its products.

We are proud and delighted to work with this beacon of sustainability, agriculture and craft. And that’s before even mentioning the excellent flavour profile of their grains!

Infinity Foods

infinityfoodswholesale.coop ↗

A local wholefoods worker cooperative since 1971, they provide a large range of ethical food items and cleaning products at fair prices and with a friendly voice.

The traceability of their products is fantastic, meaning we know the story and provenance of each and every item.

Closer to home

We also pitch in a little ourselves...

The Allotment

Abi shares an allotment in Hollingdean with some friends and we are grateful to say that it is an increasingly fruitful and important part of our operation.

They take enormous pride and joy in carefully growing, nurturing and harvesting the freshest of fresh ingredients. So far they have been growing lots of our herbs and leaves, but we are continually adding more bits and bobs to see what grows well and produces in abundance for us to be able to rely on its supply to the shop.

Foraging

We forage weekly for specialist wild ingredients which grow abundantly in our local area, always ensuring we are being careful and responsible.

The exciting part of it is how regularly the items change, helping us to notice the seasons and include them into our menu - such as our herbs, seaweed and cordial ingredients.
