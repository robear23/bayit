Good to know

Questions

Why do you need three days’ notice?

Our bagels take three days from mixing to baking. We start the dough for your order three days before you collect it, so anything shorter than that simply isn’t possible without cutting corners - and we don’t cut corners.

Do you deliver?

Most orders are collected from Unit 26 in the Open Market. If you need delivery for an office or event, choose “Delivery - please ask” on the form and we’ll let you know what we can do.

Can you cater for vegans and dietary needs?

Yes - we have vegan fillings and schmears, and can mix fish, meat-free and vegan on one order. Please note that all our products contain gluten and traces of sesame seeds.

We’re a café. How does wholesale work?

Tell us roughly how many bagels you need, which days, and whether you want them filled or plain. We’ll come back with pricing and set up a regular standing order.

Do you really do weddings?

We really do. Get in touch as early as you can with the date and numbers and we’ll talk through what would work.
